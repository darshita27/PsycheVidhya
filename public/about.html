Background

My Work & Experience

I am Vanupriya Singh, a psychology professional working in the areas of psychological counselling, psychotherapy, Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), behavioural interventions, and mental wellness. My approach is grounded in empathy, active listening, and evidence-informed psychological practices, while keeping each individual's experiences, needs, and goals at the centre of the therapeutic process.

Through my academic training and professional experience, I have worked with individuals across different age groups and with a range of emotional, behavioural, and psychological concerns — including children and adolescents, behavioural concerns, psychological assessment, counselling, and mental-wellness support.

My Approach

How I Work

Therapy should be a space where you can speak openly, without fear of judgement. At the same time, it is not only about talking — it is about understanding patterns, developing insight, learning practical skills, and working towards meaningful change.

Compassionate

Creating a safe and non-judgmental space where you feel heard and understood.

Individualised

Recognising that every person's experiences and needs are different, and adapting the therapeutic process accordingly.

Evidence-informed

Using established psychological approaches and techniques that are appropriate to your concerns and therapeutic goals.

Practical

Helping you develop coping strategies and skills that can be applied in everyday life.

Collaborative

Therapy works best as a partnership. I work with you to set goals, review progress together, and adjust the approach as your needs change.

Non-judgmental

Whatever you bring into the room — a thought, a feeling, a decision you're unsure about — is met with acceptance, not evaluation.

Confidential

What you share stays between us, within the professional and ethical boundaries of psychological practice.

Strength-based

Alongside working through difficulties, I help you notice and build on the coping resources and strengths you already have.

Getting Started

A Space to Begin

Seeking psychological support does not mean that something is “wrong” with you. Sometimes, it simply means that you do not want to navigate everything alone.

At Manova Mental Wellness, my aim is to provide a supportive, respectful, and confidential space where you can better understand your experiences, work through challenges, and develop healthier ways of moving forward.

You don't have to have everything figured out before you begin.

What to Expect

What Therapy With Me Looks Like

The first session is about understanding your concerns and what brought you here — there's no pressure to have it all figured out.

Sessions are collaborative — we'll work together to set goals that matter to you, at a pace that feels manageable.

Between sessions, you may be offered simple, practical exercises to try — only when useful to what you're working through.

Mode of therapy: in-person and online.
